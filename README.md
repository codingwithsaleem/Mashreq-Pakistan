## ✨ Overview

Mashreq Pakistan's digital banking app gives customers full control over their finances — from everyday transfers to bill payments and card management — in a clean, secure, and intuitive mobile experience built to meet the standards of a modern digital-first bank.

---

## 💼 Why Customers (and the Award Committee) Loved This

- 🔐 **Secure Biometric Login** — Fast, safe access with Face ID / fingerprint authentication
- 💸 **Instant Fund Transfers** — Send money locally and internationally in just a few taps
- 🧾 **Bill Payments & Top-Ups** — Utilities, mobile top-ups, and recurring payments handled in-app
- 💳 **Full Card Management** — Freeze/unfreeze cards, view limits, and control spending controls
- 📊 **Smart Budgeting Tools** — Spending insights and categorized transaction breakdowns
- 🏦 **Instant Account Opening** — Digital KYC that gets customers banking in minutes, not days
- 🔔 **Real-Time Alerts** — Instant notifications for every transaction and account activity
- 💬 **24/7 In-App Support** — Chat-based support built directly into the banking experience
- 🌍 **Multi-Currency Accounts** — Manage and transact across multiple currencies with ease
- 🛡️ **Bank-Grade Security** — End-to-end encryption and fraud monitoring at every layer
